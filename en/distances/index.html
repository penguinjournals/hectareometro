How many kilometres is a mile?

A mile equals 1.609 kilometres (exactly 1,609.344 metres). Conversely, a kilometre is roughly 0.62 miles — which is why metric distances often "don't add up" at first glance.

Straight-line distance examples

To get a sense of the magnitudes (approximate straight-line distances):

  • London–Manchester: about 163 miles (262 km)
  • London–Edinburgh: about 332 miles (534 km)
  • London–Paris: about 214 miles (344 km)
  • New York–Los Angeles: about 2,451 miles (3,944 km)

Click any of these figures (or type your own into the tool) and move the map: you will see at a glance how far that distance reaches from the point you choose.

Frequently asked questions about distances

How many kilometres is a mile?
A mile equals 1.609344 kilometres, i.e. about 1,609 metres. Conversely, a kilometre is roughly 0.62 miles. The distance converter turns any amount into miles, kilometres, metres, feet, yards or nautical miles.
Where do the units of distance come from?
From two opposite places: from the body and from work (the foot, the inch, the furlong, the league) or from the planet (the metre is one ten-millionth of the quadrant of the Paris meridian). The whole story is in the distance units and where they came from.
How long is a nautical mile?
A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 metres (1.852 km, 1.15 statute miles), 15 % longer than a land mile. It matches one minute of arc of a meridian, and a knot is one nautical mile per hour.
How long is a marathon?
A marathon is 26.2 miles: 26 miles and 385 yards, or 42.195 kilometres. A half marathon is exactly half of that, 13.11 miles. The figure comes from the 1908 London Olympic course and the IAAF made it official in 1921.
How far away is the horizon?
Standing on a beach, with your eyes 5 ft 7 in (1.70 m) above the ground, the horizon is 2.9 miles (4.7 km) away in a straight line, or about 3.1 miles counting the refraction of the air. The formula is d ≈ 1.22 × √h with the eye height in feet: 13 miles from a tenth-floor window and 240 miles from an airliner.

How long does it take to walk a mile?
At a normal walking pace (about 3 mph), a mile takes around 20 minutes. Three miles is roughly an hour's walk.
