How much is one trillion dollars?

One trillion dollars is 32 days of wages for the whole US, or 490 record Powerball jackpots. See it in working time, in things you know and per person.

The drawing above shows it: one trillion dollars is 32 days of wages for the whole US, or 490 record Powerball jackpots. The tool measures it two ways, by the time it takes to earn it and by things you know, with US wages and prices for 2026.

On the average wage ($69,770 a year) and spending nothing, you would have had to start work 14 million years ago, before humans existed, and never stop until today: 358,000 working lives back to back. On the federal minimum wage ($15,080 a year) it would take 66,300,000 years.

And shared out among everyone, it comes to $2,926 for every person in the United States (341.8 million people).

Careful when reading Spanish or most European languages: a trillion is a “billón” there, while their “mil millones” is the English billion. It is the most expensive translation slip in economic news.

How many zeros are in one trillion dollars?
One trillion dollars is written 1,000,000,000,000: a 1 followed by 12 zeros. A billion has nine zeros and a trillion twelve; in Spanish a “billón” is the twelve-zero one.
