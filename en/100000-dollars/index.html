How much is $100,000?

The drawing above shows it: $100,000 is 80 months on the minimum wage, or 53 months of rent. The tool measures it two ways, by the time it takes to earn it and by things you know, with US wages and prices for 2026.

On the average wage ($69,770 a year) and spending nothing, it would take you 1.4 years to earn it: starting work at 25, you would have it at 26. On the federal minimum wage ($15,080 a year) it would take 6.6 years.
