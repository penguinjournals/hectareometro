Frequently asked questions

How much is 2,500,000 litres of water?
2,500,000 litres of water is about 83.3 tanker trucks, i.e. what the population of the Balearic Islands (1.2M people) drinks in a day. In other units: 2,500 m³ or about 660,430 US gallons.
How many cubic metres are 2,500,000 litres?
2,500 cubic metres. One cubic metre is exactly 1,000 litres, so you just divide by a thousand.
How many gallons are 2,500,000 litres?
About 660,430 US gallons. One US gallon is 3.785 litres (a British imperial gallon is 4.546).
