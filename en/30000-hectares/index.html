30,000 hectares equal 300,000,000 square metres (300.00 km²) — about 42,017 football fields or 74,132 acres. But a figure like that is hard to picture: drag the map above to your city or a place you know to see these 30,000 hectares drawn at real scale.

To picture it, 30,000 hectares cover roughly the same as:

  • half the municipality of Madrid (~60,400 ha)
  • almost twice the country of Liechtenstein (~16,000 ha)
  • nearly three times the city of Paris (~10,500 ha)

Frequently asked questions

How big are 30,000 hectares?
30,000 hectares are 300,000,000 square metres (300.00 km²), about 42,017 football fields or 74,132 acres.
How many football fields fit in 30,000 hectares?
About 42,017 standard football pitches. Each pitch is 105 × 68 metres, roughly 7,140 m², i.e. 0.714 hectares — so a hectare is bigger than a football pitch, not the other way round.
How big is a square of 30,000 hectares?
A square of 30,000 hectares measures 17.32 km on each side. Drawn as a circle, which is what the Hectareometer does, it would have a radius of 9.77 km.
