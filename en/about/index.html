Who makes Hectareometer, and which part an AI does

Hectareometer is run by one person and written, for the most part, by an artificial intelligence. I think you have a right to know that before you trust a figure.

The person

My name is David González Diez. I built Hectareometer in 2017 for a simple reason: every summer the news said thousands of hectares had burned, and I could not picture how much that was. The first version did one thing: it drew a circle of N hectares on a map. I still decide what gets built and what gets published, and I am responsible for what you read here.

The AI

Since 2026 I have worked with an AI agent: Claude, made by Anthropic. It does almost all the keyboard work. It writes the articles and page copy, finds and checks the data, codes the tools and translates into English. I tell it what to do, review the result and correct the course.

I am saying this plainly so it does not look like anything else: practically all the content on this site was written by an AI, not by me.

How we try not to get things wrong

  • Figures are checked against sources before they are published. When a number is an estimate or sources disagree, the text says so.
  • Sources are noted in the site's code, which is public.
  • Conversions are not "remembered" by the AI: the code calculates them with exact factors.
  • Maps come from Google Maps and OpenStreetMap.
  • Even so, an AI makes mistakes, and so do I. We have already corrected published errors, and it will happen again.

If you spot a mistake

Write to me at dgonzalezdiez [at] gmail.com. I review it myself.
