The GTA 6 map is about 150 km²: this is how big that really is

Published on 23 November 2026

The GTA 6 map is about 150 km² (58 sq mi) with the sea and about 85 km² of land, twice GTA V. Vice City is 13 to 17 km². There is no official Rockstar figure: see it to scale over Miami, New York or London.

The GTA 6 map is about 150 square kilometres (58 square miles) including the sea, and about 85 km² (33 sq mi) of land. That is roughly twice the size of GTA V. One thing needs saying up front, though: Rockstar has not published an official figure. All the studio has given are ratios, and every square kilometre you see quoted comes from those or from maps measured by the community.

The circle on the map above is those 150 km², or 15,000 hectares, centred on downtown Miami, the real city behind Vice City. Drag it to your own city, or jump straight to it over New York, Los Angeles, London or Sydney. Grand Theft Auto VI came out on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The three sizes of the GTA 6 map

When someone says "the GTA 6 map is this big" they may mean three different things, and it pays to keep them apart:

What is measuredEstimated areaWhere it comes from
The whole map, sea included~150 km² (58 sq mi)Twice GTA V, the ratio Rockstar gives
The land of Leonida only~85 km² (33 sq mi)Community maps rebuilt before launch
Vice City alone13–17 km² (5–6.5 sq mi)Community measurements on those same maps

In other words, the 150 km² quoted everywhere is the whole map, with its keys, its swamps and a lot of ocean. Vice City itself is about ten times smaller. And none of the three has yet been measured on the released game: they are estimates from before 19 November, and we will update them when somebody does.

What Rockstar has actually said

Rockstar has only compared the map with its earlier games, in interviews with The New York Times and Edge magazine: the map is roughly twice the size of GTA V's, it has about three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2, and Vice City is twice the size of Los Santos (as reported by VGC). GTA V's map is usually put at about 76 km² with its sea, so doubling it gives the famous 150.

The land figure comes from somewhere else: the community that has spent years rebuilding the map from trailers and screenshots. Its September 2026 version put the land at 85.6 km², well below the 125 km² that was being quoted in 2024.

150 km² in things you know

  • Two and a half Manhattans (59 km²).
  • 1.6 times the real City of Miami (93 km² of land).
  • Paris and almost half as much again (the city proper is 105 km²).
  • Almost the whole of Liechtenstein (160 km²).
  • About 21,000 football pitches, or 15,000 hectares: a circle with a 6.9 km (4.3 mile) radius, or a square 12.2 km on each side.

Here is the comparison worth remembering. The real City of Miami has 93 km² of land: all the land in Leonida, six regions and all, fits in slightly less than the real Miami. And Vice City, at 13 to 17 km², is smaller than Miami Beach (20 km²) and about a sixth of the city it is modelled on. Open worlds are not big in area; they are dense. Those few square kilometres have to hold a city, a chain of keys, a swamp and a mountain.

How long it takes to cross

This part has been measured on the released game. Zack Zwiezen at Kotaku crossed Leonida from south to north four ways:

  • Walking: 3 hours 21 minutes (GTA V: about two and a half hours).
  • Running: 1 hour 41 minutes.
  • Driving, mostly on highways: 10 minutes 53 seconds (GTA V: about eight).
  • In a prop plane: 5 minutes 51 seconds.

Leonida: Florida in miniature

The state of Leonida is Rockstar's Florida, and its six regions have real models: Vice City is Miami, the Leonida Keys are the Florida Keys and Grassrivers is the Everglades, joined by Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. The scale is a model's, though: the real Everglades National Park alone is about 6,200 km² (2,400 sq mi), forty times the game's entire map.

Frequently asked questions about the GTA 6 map

How big is the GTA 6 map?
Rockstar has not published an official figure. Estimates put it at about 150 km² (58 square miles) including the sea, which is twice GTA V, and about 85 km² (33 square miles) of land according to community-built maps.
How big is Vice City in GTA 6?
The built-up area of Vice City is between 13 and 17 km² (5 to 6.5 square miles) according to community measurements. Rockstar has only said it is twice the size of Los Santos. The 150 km² often quoted is the whole map, not the city.
Is the GTA 6 map bigger than GTA 5?
Yes, roughly twice as big. GTA V's map is usually put at about 76 km² with the sea and GTA 6's at about 150 km². On land the gap is a little smaller: about 85 km² against about 49.
What is the GTA 6 map equal to in the real world?
Its 150 km² is two and a half times Manhattan, 1.6 times the City of Miami or almost the whole of Liechtenstein. Its land, about 85 km², is slightly less than the real City of Miami, which has 93 km².
How long does it take to cross the GTA 6 map?
In Kotaku's tests on the released game, south to north takes 3 hours 21 minutes on foot, 1 hour 41 minutes running, almost 11 minutes by car and under 6 minutes in a prop plane.
What real place is the GTA 6 map based on?
Florida. The state of Leonida is a scaled-down Florida: Vice City is Miami, the Leonida Keys are the Florida Keys and Grassrivers is the Everglades.
