300 hectares equal 3,000,000 square metres (3.00 km²) — about 420 football fields or 741 acres. But a figure like that is hard to picture: drag the map above to your city or a place you know to see these 300 hectares drawn at real scale.

To picture it, 300 hectares cover roughly the same as:

  • New York's Central Park (~341 ha)
  • twice London's Hyde Park (142 ha)
  • one and a half times the Principality of Monaco (202 ha)

Frequently asked questions

How big are 300 hectares?
300 hectares are 3,000,000 square metres (3.00 km²), about 420 football fields or 741 acres.
How many football fields fit in 300 hectares?
About 420 standard football pitches. Each pitch is 105 × 68 metres, roughly 7,140 m², i.e. 0.714 hectares — so a hectare is bigger than a football pitch, not the other way round.
How big is a square of 300 hectares?
A square of 300 hectares measures 1.73 km on each side. Drawn as a circle, which is what the Hectareometer does, it would have a radius of 977 metres.
